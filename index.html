Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 5, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to People Friend. We are committed to protecting the privacy and personal data of busy professionals. This privacy policy explains how our application handles information across all mobile device models.

2. Zero Cloud Data Collection Policy

To ensure complete safety from data breaches and bad actors, People Friend operates with a strict 100% On-Device execution architecture. All voice matching, signature generation, and biometric verification happen entirely on your phone’s local silicon processor. Your raw audio files are never transmitted to, processed by, or stored on external cloud servers.

3. Application Permissions & Usage

People Friend utilizes universal system protocols to securely handle hands-free tasks via native OS deep links (tel:, whatsapp://, spotify://). We do not read your private messages, notifications, or call logs.

4. Data Retention and Deactivation

Toggling the master "Agent Active" switch off instantly terminates the local microphone monitoring loop. Uninstalling the app permanently purges all local voiceprints and preference settings from the device.

Terms of Service

Last Updated: September 5, 2026

1. Anti-Hack Systems and Mandatory Rate-Limiting

To actively safeguard user devices from hackers, automated credential stuffing, and social engineering attacks, the application enforces a strict, local tracking metric limiting high-impact voice actions (such as One-Time Password processing) to a ceiling of five (5) times within a single 24-hour day. Passing this threshold results in an automated, mandatory 24-hour freeze of the automation engine.

2. Administrative Email Lock

Users are required to bind each of the maximum ten (10) allowed voice profile slots to a valid, verified recovery email address. Attempting to modify configuration settings or alter biometric databases without satisfying the email verification overlay will cause the app to lock the target profile slot automatically.
